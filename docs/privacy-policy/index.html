BP26 Badge Emulator

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

Data on your device

The app stores the emulated badge identity, credentials, settings, installed badge apps, and their files in Android WebView storage. This storage lets the badge pick up where it left off after a restart. Clearing the app’s storage in Android removes this local data.

Online features

The badge can boot offline. If you connect to Emulator Guest, the firmware uses your device’s internet connection to reach the Solana badge service. Requests can include the emulated badge ID, firmware version, device credentials, and information needed to complete actions such as app downloads and syncing. The badge service also receives standard network information such as your IP address.

If you choose to link the badge, the app opens the badge service’s claim page in your browser. The service handles account sign-in and linking under its own terms and privacy practices. Clearing local app storage does not delete data held by that service.

Installed badge apps may use online features allowed by the firmware. Review their permissions and source before installing them.

Website and contact
